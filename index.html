About

Public service, from every side of the table.

I’m Nice. For more than 15 years, I’ve worked on governance in the Philippines: inside national government, alongside civil society, and with international development partners including USAID, UNICEF and UN-affiliated bodies.

I’ve served as Chief of Staff at the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) and as Program Manager of the Capacitating LGUs on Resettlement Governance at the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG). Today I’m a Senior Lecturer at the University of the Philippines National College of Public Administration and Governance (UP NCPAG), and I work independently as a governance, policy and capacity development consultant.

I’m also a registered nurse. And as the creator of Nice to Know, I make short videos that explain Philippine laws, policies, government processes and adulting requirements in plain language.

I work in English, Filipino, Visayan and Chavacano.

Nice to Know

Government, explained simply.

Laws, policies and government processes affect every Filipino, but they aren’t always explained in a way that’s easy to follow. Nice to Know is my series of short videos that break them down: what a law actually says, how a government process works, and what you need for life’s adulting requirements.
